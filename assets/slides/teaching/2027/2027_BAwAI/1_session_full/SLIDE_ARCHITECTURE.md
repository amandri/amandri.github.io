## Teaching logic

1. S1-01–S1-04: course structure and finance refresher before baseline.
2. S1-05–S1-07: student-composed one-message baseline from a common sketch.
3. S1-08–S1-12: post-baseline stronger single-shot instruction, four-dimensional rubric and partial professional feedback.
4. S1-13–S1-17: LLM, generation, context, model/application/tool and AI-Fluency mental model.
5. S1-18–S1-26: eval-first prompting, P0–P4, examples, structured prompts, verification and evidence workpapers.
6. S1-27: redo in a different fresh ephemeral chat, maximum three messages.
7. S1-28–S1-31: full answer, tensions, instructor workflow and baseline-to-redo comparison/homework.
8. S1-32–S1-34: course continuity and Session-2 preparation.
9. S1-35–S1-54: release policy, finance/AI/reference depth and bidirectional navigation.
